# Candidate review

Reviewed 18 Research desk leads on 17 November 2026. Published 15 leads, deferred two for specific missing evidence and dropped one proposed parent-company profile. The additions comprise 17 financing vehicles and six institutions, including separate fund sleeves and documented counterparties. They add 29 sourced relationships.

The default view now contains 56 entities and 58 relationships, including eight nature-finance relationships. The full registry contains 73 entities and 73 relationships. The original 50 entities, 44 relationships and five amounts were preserved.
