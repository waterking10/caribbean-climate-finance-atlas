# Research methodology

The atlas maps the institutional relationships that support climate adaptation, mitigation, the energy transition, disaster resilience, biodiversity and nature finance, environmental sustainability, and transition finance in the Caribbean. Sources include official institutional profiles, programme and constituting documents, audited disclosures and financier records. Use project documentation to verify named participants and roles in institutional financing arrangements.

1. Identify the entity’s legal or programme name and check historical names. Record an operating institution and any named fund or facility as separate entities.
2. Record the characteristics supported by the cited sources. Apply sector, geography and ownership labels to reflect the disclosed roles. Optional fields with incomplete research stay blank.
3. Read the source for each proposed link and capture the parties, role, direction, evidence and dated context. Dated announcements support historical statements even if the present state has changed.
4. Inspect any amount separately. Identify the exact legal or programme parties, original currency, financial meaning, and whether the cited figure is a ceiling, approval, commitment or payment.
5. Review funding access in the official material. Describe engagement routes as documented and include current eligibility or deadlines only when explicitly verified.

Accreditation and implementation describe delivery roles, and partnership describes cooperation. Financial transfers require specific evidence of finance. If a disclosure identifies a trustee or intermediary, preserve that role in the description.

Geographic tags express documented coverage at the level reviewed. Country tags may be incomplete, and a broader mandate such as Caribbean does not automatically populate every country filter. The registry may omit significant relationships while source review is pending.

## Scope assessments

| Status | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| `CORE` | Primary evidence documents that an entity provides, manages, administers, prepares or shares risk for qualifying finance in the Caribbean. A targeted instrument with Caribbean eligibility, a formal delivery assignment for a funded activity or a documented operation can establish a direct role. Diversified institutions qualify through their actual roles. |
| `SUPPORTING` | Evidence documents a relationship with a core entity. Examples include an establishing body, a holding group and a project company that receives dedicated financing. A supporting entity appears in the default view when a thematic relationship explains a core entity’s financing or institutional structure. |
| `OUT_OF_SCOPE` | The reviewed record contains no documented connection to qualifying Caribbean finance. The rationale cites the material assessed. Classification can change with qualifying evidence. |

Assess legal entities and vehicles individually. Sector exposure, eligibility for accreditation, broad sustainability commitments and names containing climate or green terms are insufficient on their own. A dedicated fund with evidence of relevant Caribbean activity can qualify through its own financing window. A wider group, manager, fund and sub-fund each require their own evidence.

Review each edge independently. Retain relevant financing, risk, governance, accreditation and delivery links in the focused view, and explain how the source supports the scope decision. A core endpoint can also have general business relationships visible in Full network. Accreditation and trusteeship record the documented mechanisms, including edges with no amounts or transfer claims.

## Thematic tags

Apply every supported tag at the level of the individual entity or relationship. An entity’s tags describe its evidence-backed activities. An edge’s tags describe the financing arrangement or relevant institutional channel. Thematic filters match tagged entities and the endpoints of tagged relationships. They display only relationships carrying the selected tag. Theme totals overlap.

| Code | Apply when evidence identifies |
| --- | --- |
| `energy_transition` | Renewable generation, storage, energy efficiency, or infrastructure for a cleaner energy system. |
| `climate_mitigation` | Activity designed to avoid, reduce or remove greenhouse gas emissions. |
| `climate_adaptation` | Activity designed to reduce vulnerability to current or expected climate impacts. |
| `disaster_resilience` | Risk reduction, preparedness, contingency financing, parametric cover or recovery arrangements focused on disasters. |
| `biodiversity_nature` | Finance for ecosystem protection, restoration, wildlife or marine conservation. |
| `environmental_sustainability` | A defined environmental outcome such as pollution prevention, wastewater management or resource efficiency. |
| `transition_finance` | A financing or preparation mechanism that enables a documented shift from existing practices or infrastructure, such as capital that replaces fossil fuel reliance with renewable power or supports an explicit low-emissions transition plan. |

## Institutional roles

Apply multiple roles when supported. The existing type, category and ownership fields describe the institution’s overall operating model or legal form. Roles describe what the entity is documented to do across its portfolio. Edge participant roles identify the specific capacity in that relationship, such as lender, borrower, arranger, trustee, sponsor or policyholder.

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `dedicated_fund` | Dedicated climate/environmental fund that pools or allocates earmarked resources. |
| `thematic_vehicle` | Thematic investment vehicle with defined eligibility or investment targets. |
| `diversified_financier` | Provides finance across multiple sectors or objectives as part of its overall business model. |
| `financial_intermediary` | Arranges, channels or on-lends finance. |
| `fund_manager` | Manages or administers a vehicle or an investment portfolio. |
| `capital_provider` | Supplies documented grants, lending, equity or other capital. |
| `project_preparation` | Supplies preparation funding, feasibility work, structuring or investment-readiness support. |
| `risk_sharing` | Provides guarantees, parametric cover or another documented mechanism for sharing risk. |
| `implementing_entity` | Implements or executes a programme or funded activity. |

## Research candidate review

Start with a named institution, fund, vehicle or financing mechanism. Prioritize primary sources documenting a Caribbean operation, a named regional delivery channel or Caribbean eligibility. Check aliases and distinguish umbrella funds, sub-funds and administrators. Record which parts of the evidence describe a proposal, approval, signing, commitment, payout or historical operation. Capture the remaining verification work in the review note. When preparing a dataset update, substantiate each proposed role, edge, direction and amount separately. Research Desk code and pending leads are retained outside the public application; existing browser-local decisions must be backed up on their original origin before release (see the [backup procedure](PUBLIC_NAVIGATION_AUDIT.md)). Public suggestions use the sourced GitHub issue template. Publication uses the verified catalog and scope assessment files.

## International capital-source review

An international expansion starts with a coverage audit and a separate proposal. Establish a documented thematic Caribbean operation or financing channel for each financial claim. Record general eligibility, accreditation, strategy statements and prospective contributions as research leads. Reuse existing registry and research IDs after matching legal identities and dated names.

Trace the furthest upstream source established by evidence. A pooled institution can have documented shareholders, donors, creditors or market funding while the attribution of a particular advance remains unknown. Record institution-wide resources and operation-specific capital with their respective scopes. A donor's presence in a later pool report requires additional evidence to attribute its contribution to an earlier award.

Separate finance, administration, decisions, technical assistance and contingent risk. Record the responsible body and the specific decision stage where supported. A market arranger, fund manager or insurer can participate in several roles, each requiring its own evidence. Brand names and investor classes remain explicitly labelled until legal counterparties or membership are established.

Amounts retain their measure, stage, currency, source and date precision. Mark components, successive stages, alternative reports and overlapping umbrella facilities. A financial reporting date can remain unknown, with the access date stored separately. Flag conflicting dates, budget scopes, legal identities or financial totals for review. Source-access limitations accompany the claims they affect.

For the international expansion, the [coverage audit](INTERNATIONAL_CAPITAL_COVERAGE.md) and [publication proposal](INTERNATIONAL_CAPITAL_PROPOSAL.md) preceded canonical data edits. Following the user's approval, the approved records were integrated into the registry and pathway dataset. The [structured review package](research/international-capital-proposal.json) preserves the reviewed proposal, and the [publication manifest](research/international-capital-publication.json) records additions and baseline record fingerprints.
