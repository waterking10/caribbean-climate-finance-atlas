# International capital coverage gap report

Baseline audit dated 18 November 2026. This report covers the 73 entities, 73 relationships and 134 source records in the published registry and the separate set of 14 capital pathways. Eleven pathways are accessible from 25 registry relationships. The pathway dataset records upstream coverage as documented for three pathways, partial for ten, and unknown for one. These ratings describe the documented financing legs within each case; they do not imply a complete view of the capital behind a pooled institution.

## International actors absent from the published network

Already evidenced in pathway records are Germany's Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (`germany-bmz`), KfW (`kfw`), GIZ (`giz`), UNDP (`undp`), Credit Suisse International (`credit-suisse-international`), the BB Blue special-purpose issuer (`bb-blue-dac`), and UK Sustainable Infrastructure Programme (`uk-sip`). The MPC pathway also identifies distinct historical advisers and the sponsor vehicle. If an actor is promoted, reuse its established ID and retain the legal or programme distinction. The German government and its ministry may describe different levels of the same funded leg and should not produce duplicate contributions.

Three financing mechanisms are already in the research dataset with reusable IDs and have no published graph relationship. They are FP242 (`fp242`), the CBF Advancing Circular Economy Facility (`ace-facility`), and Barbados' catastrophe deferred drawdown option (`barbados-cat-ddo`). Their existing observations and sources should be reused if they are proposed for publication.

## Missing arrangements to prioritize

1. Trace donor-funded disaster-risk finance and investor-backed insurance separately. Priorities are the CCRIF multi-donor trust fund and the 2026 Jamaica catastrophe bond.
2. Add named, documented operations for GCF, GEF and the Adaptation Fund to supplement accreditation, trusteeship and preparation edges. Identify the eligible project, implementing institution and actual financing stage.
3. Reconcile the parallel Enertur lenders, and find dated on-lending or other downstream allocations under the EIB–CDB line.
4. Establish the donor and channel for the CBF ecosystem-based adaptation facility and distinguish it from the existing endowment and circular-economy records.
5. Look for signed co-financing and guarantee instruments in new Caribbean debt conversions, with primary evidence identifying lenders, guarantors, coverage and any philanthropic grants. Review BII and private market leads against the same standard.
