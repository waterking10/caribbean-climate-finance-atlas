The user approved the ten cases and twenty evidence-supported registry candidates on 18 November 2026, with the documented gaps retained. Publication adds their profiles, evidence and scope assessments, and links their pathway claims through stable IDs. The institutional explorer remains the main entry point. Case operations provide evidence for its financing relationships.

The default view shows 76 entities and 87 thematic relationships. All ten international cases are accessible through highlighted relationships.

The research layer contains 24 pathways, 169 sourced assertions, 65 financial observations and 64 open questions. These figures include the existing case studies. The publication maps the proposal's 52 assertions, adds five recipient-class assertions and an explicit German public-funding channel through KfW, and reuses three original canonical amounts. A registry link identifies Belize as the borrower of its blue-loan mechanism alongside the documented legal lender. The two links describe the same underlying facility. The Jamaica investor subscription identifies IBRD as the immediate issuer receiving capital.

BMZ, KfW and GIZ retain their existing research IDs as registry institutions. Cuba's geography is moved to the shared registry. The three previously staged mechanisms remain subject to their separate publication review.
