# Data sources

The [main source register](src/data/sources.json) and [scope audit sources](src/data/scope-sources.json) provide titles, publishers, URLs, access dates, publication dates where identified, and paraphrased references. Source cards in the app show these details alongside the official link.

The registry contains 37 verified institutions, 36 verified vehicles, 73 verified relationships and 134 source records. Eight relationships include a disclosed financial amount. The Research desk contains 18 reviewed leads, including 15 published candidates linked to their atlas profiles. See the [candidate review](CANDIDATE_REVIEW.md) for decisions and updated coverage.

## Examples by coverage

- Caribbean development finance and country coverage draw on the bank’s institutional description and official borrowing-member list. [CDB borrowing members](https://www.caribank.org/countries-and-members/borrowing-members)
- Climate finance delivery uses the relevant accreditation directories and project records. [Green Climate Fund accredited entities](https://www.greenclimate.fund/partners/accredited-entities/all), [Adaptation Fund implementing entities](https://www.adaptation-fund.org/apply-funding/implementing-entities/)
- Bilateral and blended infrastructure finance uses the regional programme or donor disclosure. [UK Caribbean Infrastructure Fund](https://www.caribank.org/our-work/programmes/united-kingdom-caribbean-infrastructure-fund-ukcif), [EU investment facility](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/policies/programming/programmes/lacif-eu-vehicle-generate-sustainable-investment-latin-america-and-caribbean_en)
- Commercial and institutional capital uses group disclosures and statutory scheme material. [Republic Financial Holdings subsidiaries](https://www.rfhl.com/subsidiaries/), [Barbados National Insurance Fund investments](https://www.nis.gov.bb/investments-2/), [NIBTT investments](https://www.nibtt.net/NIBTT/Investments.html)
- Private credit and equity vehicles use operating and offering materials. [Sygnus Credit Investments](https://sygnusgroup.com/alternative-investment-platform/sygnus-credit-investments/), [Sygnus Deneb Investments](https://sygnusgroup.com/alternative-investment-platform/sygnus-deneb/)
- Renewable infrastructure investment uses sponsor and lender disclosures. [InterEnergy portfolio](https://interenergy.com/portfolio/), [FinDev Canada Enertur transaction summary](https://www.findevcanada.ca/sites/default/files/2025-02/2025_001_TDS_Enertur_EN.pdf)
- Disaster risk finance uses insurer disclosures and evidence about participating governments and donor support. [CCRIF COAST coverage](https://www.ccrif.org/projects/coast/caribbean-ocean-and-aquaculture-sustainability-facility?language_content_entity=en)

## Financial observations

The original observations cover the IDB Global Credit Loan for the Eastern Caribbean energy facility, the CTF and GEF grant ceilings disclosed for that facility, EIB’s signed loan to CDB, and FinDev Canada’s individual commitment to Enertur. [IDB RG-L1071](https://www.iadb.org/en/project/RG-L1071), [CDB’s SEF financing breakdown](https://www.caribank.org/newsroom/news-and-events/cdb-signs-agreement-idb-secure-usd715m-sustainable-energy-facility-eastern-caribbean), [EIB’s CDB lending announcement](https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2024-392-global-gateway-eib-and-caribbean-development-bank-partner-to-boost-resilience-to-climate-change-in-the-caribbean), [FinDev Canada transaction summary](https://www.findevcanada.ca/sites/default/files/2025-02/2025_001_TDS_Enertur_EN.pdf)
